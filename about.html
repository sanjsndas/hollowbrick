About Hollow Brick

An independent editorial publication about interior design, architecture, and the built environment. We publish in-depth articles for people who want to understand how spaces work.

Our Story

Hollow Brick Ideas began in 2021 as a personal notebook. Maya Chen, an architectural designer, was tired of scrolling through glossy, surface-level design content that treated homes as backdrops for trends rather than places where real life unfolds. She started writing longer, slower pieces—examining why a room feels right, how materials age, and what makes a space genuinely livable.

What started as a few shared essays soon found an audience of design professionals, curious homeowners, and students who wanted more than pretty pictures. Today, Hollow Brick Ideas is an independent journal with a small editorial team and a clear mission: to bridge the gap between professional practice and everyday design curiosity.

“We believe good design is not about spectacle. It’s about the quiet choreography of light, material, and proportion that shapes how we live.”

What We Write About

We cover the full spectrum of the built environment—from residential architecture and interior design to material science, lighting strategy, and space planning. Our articles are practical, researched, and written for people who want to make better decisions about their own spaces, whether they’re renovating a kitchen, building a home, or simply trying to make a rental feel like their own.

We don’t chase trends. Instead, we focus on enduring principles: how to read a floor plan, how to choose materials that age gracefully, how to layer lighting for comfort and drama, and how to design for the way people actually live.

Our Editorial Values

  • Independence We are not sponsored by brands or swayed by advertisers. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and editorial judgment.
  • Clarity We explain complex ideas in plain language—no jargon, no gatekeeping. If a concept is worth knowing, it’s worth explaining well.
  • Depth We take the time to explore topics thoroughly. Our articles are meant to be read, not skimmed—and to be useful long after publication.
  • Honesty We acknowledge trade-offs, limitations, and the realities of budget and construction. Good design advice includes the fine print.

Meet the Team

MC

Maya Chen

Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Maya is an architectural designer and writer based in Portland, Oregon. She holds a Master of Architecture from UC Berkeley and has spent over a decade working on residential and adaptive-reuse projects. She founded Hollow Brick Ideas to create the kind of design writing she wanted to read—thoughtful, practical, and free from fluff.

JR

Jonas Riedel

Senior Editor, Materials & Craft

Jonas is a furniture maker and material researcher based in Berlin. He writes about wood, stone, metal, and the quiet beauty of things that wear well. His work has appeared in Fine Woodworking and Dwell.

SP

Simone Park

Contributing Editor, Lighting & Interiors

Simone is a lighting designer and interior stylist in Seoul. She writes our popular lighting guides and is passionate about helping people understand how light shapes the way a room feels.

Our Promise to Readers

We will always tell you when we don’t know something. We will disclose our sources. We will correct our mistakes. And we will never publish an article just to fill space. Every piece we run is meant to inform, challenge, or inspire—ideally all three.

Thank you for reading. If you have a question, a story idea, or just want to say hello, please get in touch.
