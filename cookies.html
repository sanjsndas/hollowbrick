    How we use cookies and similar technologies on Hollow Brick Ideas.

    Last updated: March 15, 2025

    Effective date: March 15, 2025

    This Cookies Policy explains how Hollow Brick Ideas ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website hollowbrickideas.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by another domain, such as an analytics service).

    In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies such as web beacons (tiny graphics), pixel tags, and local storage to collect and store information about your browsing activity.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential functionality: To enable core site features such as page navigation, security, and accessibility.
    • Preferences: To remember choices you make (e.g., language, display preferences) and provide a more personalized experience.
    • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with the Site — which pages are most popular, how long people stay, and how they arrive. This helps us improve our content and user experience.
    • Newsletter and form handling: To manage newsletter signups and contact form submissions securely.

    We do not use cookies for targeted advertising or to sell your data to third parties.

    3. Types of Cookies We Use

    The table below describes the specific cookies that may be set when you visit our Site. Please note that third-party cookies are controlled by their respective providers, and their availability may change.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    hbi_session Essential Maintains your session state across page requests and ensures basic site functionality. Session
    hbi_prefs Preference Stores your cookie consent choice and any display preferences you set. 12 months
    _ga, _gid Analytics Set by Google Analytics to distinguish users and track site usage anonymously. We use this data to improve our content. 2 years / 24 hours
    _gat Analytics Used by Google Analytics to throttle request rates and limit data collection on high-traffic sites. 1 minute
    mailchimp_* Functional Set by our newsletter provider (Mailchimp) to manage subscription forms and prevent spam. Varies (up to 1 year)
    fonts_loaded Preference Remembers that Google Fonts have loaded successfully to avoid repeated requests. Session

    Note: We do not use any advertising or retargeting cookies. If we ever introduce such cookies in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent where required.

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and they are subject to the privacy policies of the respective third parties. The main third-party services we use are:

    • Google Analytics: For anonymous traffic analysis. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Mailchimp: For newsletter subscription management. See Mailchimp's Cookie Statement.
    • Google Fonts: To display typography. Google may set a cookie to track font usage; see Google's Privacy Policy.
    • Unsplash: Images are loaded from Unsplash's CDN, which may set cookies for performance and analytics. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.

    5. Your Cookie Choices

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. When you first visit our Site, you will see a cookie banner where you can manage your preferences. You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

    • Browser controls: Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies. The methods vary by browser — check your browser's help menu for instructions.
    • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of analytics cookies using the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool or the Your Online Choices platform (EU).
    • Do Not Track: Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, but you can manage tracking via the methods above.

    Please note that disabling certain cookies (especially essential ones) may affect the functionality of the Site. For example, you may not be able to submit forms or access certain features.

    6. Consent and Legal Basis

    For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we rely on your consent to set non-essential cookies (such as analytics and preference cookies). You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your cookie settings or contacting us. Essential cookies are set on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing a functional website.

    For users in other regions, we comply with applicable local laws regarding cookies and similar technologies.

    7. Updates to This Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will notify you via a prominent notice on the Site and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
