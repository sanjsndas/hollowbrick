    Before you write

    Do you accept guest posts?

    Rarely. We only publish contributed articles from established design professionals. Send a short pitch first — no full drafts.

    Can you review my product?

    We do not accept unsolicited product samples. For review consideration, please email a press release and high-res images to our editorial address.

    I found a factual error.

    Thank you — we appreciate corrections. Please include the article URL and the specific correction in your message.

    Response time

    We're a small team. Most emails get a reply within 2–3 business days. If you don't hear back within a week, feel free to follow up.
